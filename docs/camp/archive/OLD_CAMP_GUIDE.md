# Simple Guide: Managing Camp, Groups, and Rooms

Welcome! This quick 3-step guide shows you how to set up a new camp, upload your attendees or groups, and assign rooms.

---

## Step 1: Create a New Camp Meeting

1. Open the Admin Portal sidebar and click **My Camps** (or click the Camp Switcher at the top of the sidebar and select **+ Create New Camp**).
2. Click the **+ New Camp** button.
3. Fill in:
   - **Camp Name**: e.g., `TACC Youth Camp 2026`
   - **Public URL Slug**: Automatically generated (e.g., `tacc-youth-camp-2026`).
   - **Camp Logo (Optional)**: Click to upload your camp logo image (`PNG`, `JPG`, `WebP`).
   - **Room Categories**: Set default categories like `Villa`, `Hostel`, `Dormitory`, or add custom types.
4. Click **Create Camp**. The system automatically sets your new camp as the **Active Camp** across the portal.

---

## Step 2: Add Groups & Attendees (CSV Import)

### Option A: Import Groups via CSV (Recommended)
1. Go to **Groups** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Import Groups CSV**.
3. Select your CSV file containing attendee details.
4. The system automatically:
   - Filters out header labels (like `MALES`, `FEMALES`, `PAIRINGS`, `SECTION`).
   - Detects PFCC badges (e.g., `PFCC 1`, `PFCC 2`).
   - Pairs roommates into groups according to their room preference.
5. Click **Import Groups**.

### Option B: Add Individual People
1. Go to **People** in the sidebar.
2. Click **+ Add Person** (or **Import CSV** for raw attendee lists).
3. Fill in Full Name, Fellowship, Gender, Day of Arrival, Phone number, and PFCC badge.

---

## Step 3: Assign Rooms to Groups & People

### Method A: Click-to-Assign Side Panel (Easiest)
1. Go to **Groups** in the sidebar.
2. Click on any **Group Card** (e.g., *Group 1*).
3. A right-side panel opens showing all group members and room options:
   - **Select an Existing Room**: Pick a room from the list (matching room type preference appears first).
   - **Add & Assign New Room**: Enter a new room number (e.g. `H-305`), set room type, and click **Create & Assign Room**.
4. The room assignment instantly syncs across the group and all members.

### Method B: Visual Room Assignment Board
1. Go to **Groups** → click **Assign Rooms** at the top.
2. Click an unassigned group on the left panel.
3. Click any available room on the right panel to assign them immediately.

### Method C: Key Bearer Assignment
1. Go to **Rooms** in the sidebar.
2. Select any room to expand its occupants.
3. Click the **Key icon** next to an occupant's name to assign them as the room's Key Holder. They will be highlighted with a blue **Key Holder** badge in real time.

---

## Direct SMS Notifications
- On any person's detail sidebar or room list, click the **Send SMS** button to send instant room details (Room Number, Room Type, Key Holder name) directly to their phone number.
