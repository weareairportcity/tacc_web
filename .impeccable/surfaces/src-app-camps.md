# Surface brief: Commune attendee pages (/camps/*)

Mode: Operate (the camp page leans Persuade: one decision, register).
Audience: church members on phones from a WhatsApp/SMS link. Task: register, pay in instalments, see status, choose roommates, see room.
Constraints: mobile-first 390px; GHS; Paystack checkout is external; 8-char sign-in code, no passwords.
Pinned visual world: Seline-style "quiet analyst's desk on warm paper": stone canvas #fafaf9, white 10px hairline cards, Ink #0c0a09 text, one cyan (#3ba6f1) for the single primary action, pill controls, Inter Tight 400 display and Inter body. It replaced the cobalt world on 24 Oct 2026.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Calm certainty about money and next steps; the attendee's "pass" is the one elevated object, and everything else is quiet paper and hairlines. It refuses loud event-ticketing hype.
OWN-WORLD: Warm stone paper, white hairline cards with a whisper shadow, Inter Tight weight 400 headlines with one Sky Wash highlight keyword each, pill buttons, and one cyan pill per screen with ink text. Soot pills mark the active step.
STORY: You can see the camp, register in one short form, and always know exactly what you've paid, what's left, and what comes next.
FIRST VIEWPORT: The camp name as a whisper-weight headline, dates and venue, then a full-width cyan Register pill within thumb reach.
SIGNATURE: A four-step journey track (Register, Pay, Roommates, Room) whose current step is a Soot pill, repeated on the pass.
MEMORABLE MOMENT: The pass card on My registration: the balance left in large Inter Tight, lifted off the paper by the page's only deep shadow.
